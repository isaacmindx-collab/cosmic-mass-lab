## What was built
- `engine/schemas/stamp.schema.json` (JSON Schema draft 2020-12). Two forms:
  - **one run** (the runner's stamp): every key of section 2 required (`scenario`, `run_id`, `engine_commit`, `t0_status`, `law`, `universe`, `matter`, `numerics`, `environment`, `kind`, `preregistered`, `producer`), no unknown keys; the law block takes exactly the values of `LawSpec` (L0, L1a-L1e and L2-L8); `universe` the settings of `settings.py`; `run_id` is `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS-<commit>`.
  - **a test with cases** (`gates.t0.write_gate_result`, used by T0, T9, D21): the header plus `cases`, each a one-run stamp.
- `engine/schemas/summary.schema.json`. Two forms:
  - **one run** (the runner): `conservation` with the drifts of mass, charge, energy and momentum (section 4), `convergence` (object or null), `verdict` and `explanation`.
  - **a test with cases**: `verdict` in pass / fail / finding, `explanation`, `cases`.
- `tools/validate_results.py`: `validate(data, name)`, `validate_folder(folder)` and a command line, `python tools/validate_results.py [folders]`. It picks the form first (by the key `cases`), so that a message reads `cases.a.universe.dimension: 4 is not one of [2, 3]` instead of "not valid under any of the given schemas". It lives in `tools/`, not in the package: the web universe loads every file of `engine/cosmicmass/` (`simulator/web/engine-files.json`), and must not need `jsonschema`.
- `engine/tests/test_schemas.py` (21 tests): every committed result folder is checked; fresh outputs of the runner and of `write_gate_result` validate; invalid stamps and summaries fail with the expected messages; the command line.
- `jsonschema==4.26.0` in `requirements.txt` and `pyproject.toml` (D13; `test_pins.py` keeps them equal). Its own dependencies (attrs, referencing, jsonschema-specifications, rpds-py) are not pinned, as for pytest.

## Results
Software facts only; no finding.

**Found while building:** the summaries of D21.1 to D21.4 (`results/D21.*/`) have verdicts written as sentences ("supports D21 (1)", "refines D21 (3): report both", ...), but section 4 of the standard allows `pass`, `fail` or `finding`. Everything else in them is valid. Following `AGENTS.md` (do not fix someone else's result silently; results are written by scripts, never by hand), they are **not** edited: the test lists them as known exceptions, checks that the verdict is their only problem, and issue #24 reports them. The producer can re-run `scenarios/D21/run.py` with a standard verdict (and the sentence moved to `explanation`), then remove them from `KNOWN_NONCONFORMING`.

## Open questions (for the verification lead, who owns the standard)
1. **Plain runs:** the runner writes `verdict: "none"` ("a plain run has no pass criterion"). Section 4 lists only pass, fail and finding. The schema accepts `none` for the one-run form only, as a labelled assumption, because it is what the engine writes; tests with cases must use pass, fail or finding. Should the standard name `none`?
2. **Tests with cases:** section 4 asks every summary for conservation drifts and convergence. Gate tests that evaluate forces once (T9.1-T9.4) have neither, and the T0 cases keep them inside each case. The schema does not require them at the top level of the cases form. Should the standard say so?
3. The runner does not yet validate what it writes (task E7.2, "outputs validate against the P4 schemas"). That would make the engine import `jsonschema`, and the web universe would have to load it too; I left it to E7.2 and decision D22.
