## Part A (this pull request): L1b, L1c, L1d and L7

### Definitions (labelled choices)
1. **One rule for every reading:** force = L0's `Re[mu (E_cal + v x B_cal)]` plus `+/- lambda` (L2, L3) times what the reading adds to it. For L1a this is `docs/05`'s formula (`Re + lambda s Com`). It is the only rule under which the reduce-to-L0 test of the work plan can hold for every law: read literally, `docs/05`'s "L1c: force = Ht(mu E_cal)" drops the Real part, and a pure mass would then feel **no gravity at all**.
2. With `C` the selected Composer terms of `mu E_cal` (option L5) and `R = Re(mu E_cal)`, what each reading adds:
   - **L1a:** `s C` (unchanged);
   - **L1b:** `s cos(phi_bar) C`, which is `Sh - Re`;
   - **L1c:** `s sin(phi_bar) C`, which is `Ht`;
   - **L1d:** `|A| u - R`, where `Sh = R + s cos(phi_bar) C` and `Ht = s sin(phi_bar) C` are vectors, `|A| = sqrt(|Sh|^2 + |Ht|^2)` is the length of the vector `mu E_cal`, and `u` is the unit vector of `Sh` (of `C` if `Sh` is zero). At lambda = 1 the force is `|A| u`: "abs along the field". **Lengths of vectors, not of components**, so that the force does not depend on the orientation of the axes. For a body whose Composer coordinate is zero (a neutral mass in gravity) L1d adds nothing.
3. **L7 (E3.4):** `Re(mu) = m`; `Sh(mu) = m + q s cos(phi_bar)`; `abs(mu) = sqrt(Sh^2 + Ht^2)`. `Sh(mu)` can be negative (an electron in SI: -3.3e-10 kg); kept as the hypothesis says (T8 explores it). A zero inertia is refused by the integrator (existing). L0 takes no option, as before.

## Part B: L4, L6, L8 (labelled choices)
- **L4, direction of what the reading adds:** "radial" (as computed: along the field); "perpendicular" (turned +90 deg in the plane of motion; about z in 3D, the z component kept); "velocity" (its length, along the body's velocity; nothing for a body at rest; the sign of L2 can reverse it).
- **L6, moving bodies:** `Com(mu (v x B_cal)) = m (v x b) + q (v x B_g) + 2 s cos(phi_bar) q (v x b)` is added to the selected terms ("on") or used alone ("only"); L1d reads the Real part of the same product. The Boris push reads only the Real part, so a run with L6 on uses velocity Verlet (Boris refuses it, with a clear error).
- **L8, screened range:** the static Composer terms come from the field of the bodies screened over a length `L` with the kernel `exp(-r/L)(1 + r/L)/r^2` (the force of a Yukawa potential); L0's Real part is never screened; the uniform background is not screened. `L` is the **Debye length of the population**, `sqrt(k_B T / (4 pi K sum_i n_i q_i^2))`, recomputed at each step: `T` the kinetic temperature of the moving bodies (E6b), densities from the box that bounds the alive bodies (in 2D, bodies of 3D space in a plane, D21: area times the mean spacing `sqrt(area/N)`). No temperature, no charge or a single body: no screening. Computed by the solver (`DirectSum.at_bodies_screened`), so with the same softening as the rest, and passed in `FieldSample.screened` by the `StepContext` only for a law that asks for it.
- Tests: L6 by hand; L4 in 2D and 3D; the reduce-to-L0 test over every direction x moving option, bit for bit; L8: the Debye length by hand (2D and 3D, and its infinite cases), the screened kernel of two bodies, the Composer coupling screened while L0 is not, strength 0 gives L0, a cold population is not screened.

### L1e (labelled choices)
`docs/05`: "`mu a = mu (E_cal + v x B_cal)` as a General Number equation, so `a = E_cal + v x B_cal` (motion in two coordinates)".
1. The body's place is a General Number `X = x + xc G`. Its acceleration is `a = (g + v x B_g) + (e + v x b) G`: **the cosmic mass has dropped out**, so every body has the same General Number acceleration. `x` accelerates with `Re(a)`, `xc` with `Com(a)`.
2. **With the strength (the one rule of this notebook):** Real acceleration = L0's + `lambda (Re(a) - L0's)`; Composer acceleration = `lambda Com(a)`. So lambda = 1 is the law as written, and lambda = 0 is L0 with the Composer coordinate at rest (the reduce-to-L0 test).
3. The fields are those at the **Real positions** `x`, and `v` is the **Real velocity**, as written. (Alternatives for the physics lead: the General Number velocity `v + vc G` in `v x B_cal`; fields at another reading of `X`.)
4. L1e has no Composer force, so the options direction, terms, moving and range do not apply and are refused; sign, strength and inertia apply (at lambda = 1 the inertia cancels; the engine still divides by it, so a body of zero inertia stays refused: T8.1 may lift this).
5. **An identity (mathematics, tested on random states to 1e-13):** `Re(mu a) / Re(mu)` is L0's acceleration. So L0 is the Real reading of `mu a`, and under L1e the reading `Re(mu X) / Re(mu) = x - s^2 (q/m) xc` of the body's place follows L0 in uniform fields (tested). What L1e changes is which coordinate is taken as the place: read as `x`, **a charge under L1e at lambda = 1 does not feel the electric field at all** in its Real coordinate; the whole electric motion is in `xc`. Whether the place is `x` or a reading of `X` is the physics lead's question (question 25).

### The direction "torque" (labelled choice)
What the reading adds does not push the body; it acts at the body's surface and turns its spin: torque `+/- lambda R |added|` about the axis normal to the plane of motion (z; along z in 3D, like "perpendicular"). `R` is the body's radius; a point body gets none. The spin is an angular momentum; it does not act back on the motion.
