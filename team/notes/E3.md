## Part 1: the pair guard of D29

**Labelled interpretation of D29** (posted in the chat for correction): question 14 asked whether to adopt the nearest-neighbour rule (Q4's rule B) as the default, with the guard as its condition; D29 adopts "the pair guard of Q5 (rule C)", and rule C is B plus the guard. So:
- the default `speed_reference` becomes `"neighbour"` (in `StepContext` and in the settings defaults);
- the pair guard `dt <= eta min_(i<j) r_ij / |v_i - v_j|` (function `pair_approach_time`) is applied whatever the reference, before `dt_min` and `dt_max`; fixed bodies count as at rest;
- `"absolute"` stays as an explicit option, so scenarios written for D21 can ask for it.

If the maintainer meant only "add the guard to the absolute rule", one default value changes back; nothing else.

**Tests:** the default; `pair_approach_time` on a hand case (fixed bodies at rest, dead bodies left out, a lone body infinite); on the trap of Q5 at closest approach the step is exactly `eta r / |v|` = 5e-6, while the nearest-neighbour conditions alone (guard switched off) allow more than 20 times longer. Three calls of an older unit test that meant the absolute rule by `context()` now say `speed_reference="absolute"`; what they check is unchanged.

## Part 2: the Boris push (E3.2)

- `Boris(VelocityVerlet)`: the acceleration is split as `a = a_E(x) + v x Omega(x)`, with `Omega = (m B_g - s^2 q b) / inertia` (the Real coordinate of `mu (v x B_cal)`, L0's: `m v x B_g + q v x B`, `B = -s^2 b`). A step is velocity Verlet whose two half kicks are each a **Boris push** over half the step (quarter kick with `a_E`, Boris turn, quarter kick).
- **A first version failed its own test, and was changed** (before any delivery): it split the step as "half kick, half turn, drift, half turn, half kick". A charge launched at the drift velocity `E x B / B^2` then wobbled by 1.25e-7 of its speed (`theta^2 / 8` for a turn of `theta` = 1e-3 per step). The Boris push keeps that drift exactly at any step, so each half kick became a full Boris push; the drift test then holds to 1e-9 over 50 s.
- The stored acceleration after a step is recomputed for the final velocity (`StepContext.remember`): velocity Verlet keeps the one computed with the half-step velocity, which is right without a second field but not with one.
- Settings: `numerics.integrator` is `"verlet"` or `"boris"`; left out, D10 picks Boris when a background `B_g` or `b` is not zero. An explicit choice is kept. The stamp records the integrator that ran.
- Scope (labelled): only the Real coordinate of `mu (v x B_cal)` is a force in every implemented law; a law with L6 on is refused by `Boris.step`. The fields of moving bodies (E2.2) do not exist yet, so the second field comes from backgrounds only.
- **Tests** (`engine/tests/test_boris.py`, 7): gyration of a proton in B = 5 nT, 2D and 3D, one period: radius within 1e-6, speed constant to 1e-12, back at the start within 1e-6 of the circumference (the done-when of E3.2); E x B drift kept to 1e-9; a neutral mass turns in `B_g` with `Omega = B_g`; without a second field Boris equals velocity Verlet to rounding (1e-14); `boris_rotation` keeps the speed and turns by `2 atan(Omega h / 2)`; settings and refusals.
