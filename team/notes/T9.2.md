## Pre-registration (commit before any production run)
Committed before any code of this test was written or run. No exploratory run was made. The numbers below come from the formulas only (arithmetic with the constants of `engine/cosmicmass/constants.py`), not from the engine.

- **Question:** under the hypothesis L1a with the charge-share term only (L5c: `-2 cos(phi_bar) q E`, sign +, lambda = 1, radial), (A) is the pull between a proton and an electron Coulomb's (with Newton's) times `f = 1 - 2 cos(phi_bar)` at every separation, and so (B) does a pair set on a circle with the speed of that weaker pull stay on it with the period that pull predicts; and (C) what binding energy does the book's scaling then give?
- **Law(s):** L1a, a **hypothesis** (D1), `terms = "-2 cos q E"`, lambda = 1, sign +. The Real part is L0. The Composer force on the electron is `lambda s (2 s cos(phi_bar) q e) = -2 cos(phi_bar) q E`.
- **Settings (as in stamp.json):** SI, 2D, `phi_bar` = 79.86 deg, softening 0 (D21).
  - **(A) forces once**, no integration: a proton and an electron at rest at separations 0.5, 1, 2 and 5 Bohr radii (`a0 = 5.29177e-11` m, as in T0.1b).
  - **(B) one orbit**, integrated by the runner (velocity Verlet, adaptive step of D10 with D21's conventions): the pair in its centre-of-mass frame at `a0`, relative speed `sqrt(k / (mu_r a0))` with `k = G_N m_p m_e + f K e^2` and `mu_r` the reduced mass; `t_end` one predicted period; `eta` = 1e-3, then 5e-4 and 2.5e-4 for convergence.
- **Prediction and why (analytic):**
  - At 79.86 deg, `2 cos(phi_bar)` = 0.352108 and `f` = **0.647892**. Newton's share of the pull is 4.4e-40 of Coulomb's, far below double precision, so `f` is the ratio of the total pull to the Real pull to rounding, at every separation (the share is `1/r^2` like Coulomb's field).
  - (B) period `2 pi sqrt(mu_r a0^3 / k)` = **1.88767e-16 s**, 1.24236 times the L0 period 1.51942e-16 s; speed 1.76139e6 m/s; the separation stays `a0`.
  - (C) the book's scaling (the binding energy of hydrogen grows with the square of the pull, Bohr 1913, as cited by the book): `f^2 x 13.598 eV` = 0.419764 x 13.598 = **5.708 eV** (book: 5.71 eV), against the measured 13.598 eV (Sansonetti 2005, as cited by the book): 42% of it.
- **Analysis plan (quantities, how pass / fail / finding is judged):**
  - (A) from the engine (field solver and law): `f(r)` = (Real + Composer force on the electron, along the line to the proton) / (Real force on it, same direction). **Pass** if `f` equals `1 - 2 cos(phi_bar)` to 1e-12 at all four separations (the same number at every separation: the pull stays `1/r^2`), and the Composer force on the electron points away from the proton (it pushes back).
  - (B) period measured from the swept angle, as in T0.1; **pass** if within 0.1% of the predicted period (the T0 tolerance for periods) and the separation stays within 1e-3 of `a0`; convergence: the period at `eta/2` and `eta/4` changes by less than 1% (RESULTS-STANDARD). The energy reported by the runner is the L0 energy, which is not a conserved quantity of this law (`team/notes/MVP-7.md`): it is reported, not judged.
  - (C) **pass** if `f^2 x 13.598` eV, with `f` from the engine, rounds to the book's 5.71 eV (within 0.005 eV). Reported: its ratio to the measured 13.598 eV and the gap in eV, so the reading is **excluded** at lambda = 1, as the book states.
  - Also reported (not part of the verdict): `f` at 90 deg (expected 1: the share vanishes) and at the mirror angle 10.14 deg (expected -0.969: the share would turn Coulomb's pull into a push, and hydrogen would not be bound at all); and the largest lambda for which `(1 - 2 lambda cos(phi_bar))^2 x 13.598` stays within a given distance of 13.598 eV is **not** computed: the book gives no measurement uncertainty for this comparison, so no bound on lambda is claimed.
  - Kind: `measurement-check`, as for T9.1 and T9.3. Step (C) uses the book's scaling argument, not a quantum calculation by the engine (the engine is a classical universe of cosmic masses); the summary says so.
