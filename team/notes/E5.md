## E5.4: what was built (labelled choices)
- `populations.py`, `generate(spec, d, constants, rng, real)`: shapes `box` (uniform; the universe's box by default), `shell` (uniform in volume in 3D; an annulus, uniform in area, in 2D), `ring` (an annulus of the plane z = 0); velocities: a `bulk` flow, a **thermal spread** at `temperature` (each component normal with variance `k_B T / m`), a **Keplerian** circular speed `sqrt(G_N M / r)` about a `centre` (counter-clockwise in the plane).
- **Super-particles:** `weight` multiplies the mass and the charge, so the charge-to-mass ratio, and the acceleration in a field, stay those of the real particle; the thermal spread uses the **real particle's** mass, so a super-particle of protons at 1e4 K moves like a proton at 1e4 K.
- Settings: a top-level list `populations`, generated after the listed `bodies` (which may now be empty if there are populations). One generator seeded from `numerics.seed`, as `default_rng((seed, 1))`, a stream of its own: the environment's (E6b) is `default_rng(seed)`. The populations, as given, go into `meta` and the stamp (schema updated), only when used; with the seed they rebuild the same bodies.
- Tests (`engine/tests/test_populations.py`): **same seed, identical arrays; another seed, different ones**; **10^4 protons at 1e4 K: the kinetic temperature within 1%** (the expected statistical scatter is `sqrt(2/(3N))` = 0.8%; the seed is fixed, so the test is deterministic); each component's spread within 2%; the shapes (bounds; uniform in volume: the mean of r^3); Keplerian speeds exact to 1e-12, perpendicular and counter-clockwise; the bulk flow; super-particles; a run with listed bodies and populations whose stamp validates; 13 refused settings.
