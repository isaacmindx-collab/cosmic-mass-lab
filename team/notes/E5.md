## E5.4: what was built (labelled choices)
- `populations.py`, `generate(spec, d, constants, rng, real)`: shapes `box` (uniform; the universe's box by default), `shell` (uniform in volume in 3D; an annulus, uniform in area, in 2D), `ring` (an annulus of the plane z = 0); velocities: a `bulk` flow, a **thermal spread** at `temperature` (each component normal with variance `k_B T / m`), a **Keplerian** circular speed `sqrt(G_N M / r)` about a `centre` (counter-clockwise in the plane).
- **Super-particles:** `weight` multiplies the mass and the charge, so the charge-to-mass ratio, and the acceleration in a field, stay those of the real particle; the thermal spread uses the **real particle's** mass, so a super-particle of protons at 1e4 K moves like a proton at 1e4 K.
- Settings: a top-level list `populations`, generated after the listed `bodies` (which may now be empty if there are populations). One generator seeded from `numerics.seed`, as `default_rng((seed, 1))`, a stream of its own: the environment's (E6b) is `default_rng(seed)`. The populations, as given, go into `meta` and the stamp (schema updated), only when used; with the seed they rebuild the same bodies.
- Tests (`engine/tests/test_populations.py`): **same seed, identical arrays; another seed, different ones**; **10^4 protons at 1e4 K: the kinetic temperature within 1%** (the expected statistical scatter is `sqrt(2/(3N))` = 0.8%; the seed is fixed, so the test is deterministic); each component's spread within 2%; the shapes (bounds; uniform in volume: the mean of r^3); Keplerian speeds exact to 1e-12, perpendicular and counter-clockwise; the bulk flow; super-particles; a run with listed bodies and populations whose stamp validates; 13 refused settings.

**What was built** (engine component: no pre-registration, no result)
- `engine/cosmicmass/universe.py`: `Universe`, a frozen object with the settings U1 to U7. `Universe.from_dict` is the validation that was inside `load_settings`, moved, with the same messages; `to_dict()` gives back the dictionary that `Settings.universe` has always been, so no reader of that dictionary changes (the runner, the stamp, the gates, the scenarios).
- The done-when of E5.2, "every U option in `05-scenarios-and-tests.md` can be expressed; invalid combinations are refused": one test walks the table of U1 to U8 option by option, and one test has 28 invalid universes, each refused with the name of the bad field, directly and through the loader.

| Setting | How it is expressed |
|---|---|
| U1 frame angle, and the sweep | `phi_bar_deg`; `angle_sweep()` gives the 33 universes from 10 to 170 degrees |
| U2 dimension | `dimension` 2 or 3. "2D slice of 3D" is what dimension 2 means since D21: motion in a plane with the `1/r^2` field |
| U3 units, side by side | `units`; `in_units()` gives the twin in the other units |
| U4 boundary | `boundary`, `box`: every boundary of `Environment.BOUNDARIES` |
| U5 background fields | `background` |
| U6 fixed sources | **new:** `universe.sources`, a list of pinned cosmic masses with their places. They become fixed bodies after the listed ones. A body with `"fixed": true` still works |
| U7 inflow | `inflow` (and `heat_bath`) |
| U8 population state | the top-level `populations` of the settings (E5.4) |

- The rest of E5.3: `"law"` may be a name, `"reference"` (L0) or `"exploratory"` (the default exploratory law of `docs/05`, checked in a test against the sentence of the test plan). `Settings.world` is the `Universe`.

**Choices I made (labelled; for the maintainer)**
1. **Wrap-around (U4) is not expressible yet**, on purpose: the universe accepts exactly the boundaries the environment implements, so it will accept "wrap" the day D36 adds it to `Environment.BOUNDARIES`, with no change here. Until then it is refused, with the list of what exists. This is the one U option of the table that cannot be expressed today.
2. **U8 stays outside the object.** Populations are matter, generated with the run's seed; they need the universe (its box, its dimension) but are not part of it. The class diagram of the architecture said "populations" under U1-U8; the registry row now says where each setting lives.
3. **No law given still means L0** (question 29). The plan's words "defaults (default exploratory law)" could be read as "a file without a law runs L1a". I did not do that: it would change the meaning of every existing settings file and let a hypothesis run by omission.
4. **`in_units` does not convert numbers.** A box of one metre is not a box of one scaled length. A universe with a box, fields, sources, an inflow or a heat bath is refused, with the reason.
5. **The stamp is unchanged.** Sources appear in its `matter` list, like any body; the `universe` block of the stamp holds the same keys as before.
