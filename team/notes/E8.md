## What was built (labelled choices)
- **E8.1:** every measurement row has the `temperature` of the moving bodies (E6b's `kinetic_temperature`: `sum m v^2 / (d N k_B)`). The runner keeps the size of the momentum each wall takes, and the summary gains `walls`: per wall (`"<axis>-<side>"`) its momentum and pressure (momentum / time / size of the wall: a length in 2D, an area in 3D), and the mean over the whole boundary. In 2D (bodies moving in a plane, D21) the pressure is a force per unit length, to compare with `n k T` for `n` per unit area. Absorbed bodies count with the momentum along the wall's axis.
- **E8.2:** `analysis/convergence.py`: `convergence(settings, measure, counts=(), resize=None)`. "dt/2" is `eta/2` with the adaptive step (D10), `dt_initial/2` with a fixed step. Other body counts go through the scenario's own `resize(settings, n)`, because only the scenario knows how its population is built. The block: the measured quantities at each step, the relative changes from step to step/2 and step/2 to step/4, the changes with the count, the largest change, and `converged` (below 1%, the standard's default; a scenario may tighten it).
- **E8.3:** `analysis/plots.py`, plain SVG with the standard library (no plotting library in the pinned environment: my proposed answer to question 5 is "none is needed"). `figures(folder)` makes, from the folder alone and byte for byte the same each time: `drifts.svg` (the relative change of the Real energy and of the mass against time), `trails.svg` (the bodies in the Real plane, when the time series has positions), `mu_plane.svg` (each kind of body as the direction of its cosmic mass in the General Number plane, at the run's frame angle, with the mass axis and the Composer unit drawn). `line_plot` and `scatter_plot` for sweeps.

## Results (software checks; no finding)
- **Kinetic theory comes out:** 300 light neutral bodies in a 10 x 10 reflecting box (scaled units, gravity negligible), 2,000 steps: the mean pressure equals `N k_B T / A`, with the temperature measured by the engine, within 5%, and each wall within 15% (fewer hits each); more than 5,000 wall hits.
- The convergence helper on an eighth of a circular orbit: converged, changes below 1e-6, shrinking with the step; with body counts through `resize`; with a fixed step; and not converged when the tolerance is set below what the step allows.
- The figures of a run are made again byte for byte; a run with an inflow (no positions in its time series) gets no trails.
