## The question

The compendium gives the field equations F1-F4 with General Number fields and sources, but not the field of a moving point body, so the engine has no second field `B_cal = B_g + b G` from the bodies themselves; only uniform backgrounds. Question 11 asks for that field in the slow-body form. It blocks E2.2, the tests T5, and gives the option L6 and the Boris push (E3.2) something to act on besides a background.

## The derivation (mathematics)

The field equations of the compendium ("Cosmic Field Law and law of motion"), with `rho_mu = rho_m + rho_q G` and `J_mu = J_m + J_q G`:

    div E_cal = -4 pi G rho_mu,     div B_cal = 0,
    curl E_cal = -dB_cal/dt,        curl B_cal = -(4 pi G / c^2) J_mu + (1/c^2) dE_cal/dt.

Take one body of cosmic mass `mu` at `r_s(t)`, moving with velocity `v` small compared with `c`. To zeroth order in `v/c` its field is the field at rest, carried along with it:

    E_cal(r, t) = -G mu (r - r_s) / |r - r_s|^3,     with dE_cal/dt = -(v . grad) E_cal.

**Claim.** To first order in `v/c`,

    B_cal = (1/c^2) v x E_cal.

**Proof.** `div B_cal = (1/c^2) div(v x E_cal) = -(1/c^2) v . curl E_cal = 0`, because the field at rest has no curl. And, with `v` constant,

    curl(v x E_cal) = v (div E_cal) - (v . grad) E_cal = -4 pi G mu v delta(r - r_s) + dE_cal/dt,

so `curl B_cal = -(4 pi G / c^2) mu v delta + (1/c^2) dE_cal/dt`, which is the fourth equation with the current `J_mu = mu v delta` of a point body. The third equation, `curl E_cal = -dB_cal/dt`, holds only up to terms of order `(v/c)^2`: that is what "slow-body form" means, and it is the order at which the compendium's law is stated (slow bodies only).

**In the two coordinates** (`E_cal = g + e G`, `B_cal = B_g + b G`, `mu = m + q G`; the relation is linear, so each coordinate follows its own):

    B_g = (1/c^2) v x g,        b = (1/c^2) v x e,

and with `E = -s^2 e`, `B = -s^2 b`:

    B = (1/c^2) v x E = (mu_0 / 4 pi) q v x r_hat / r^2,     mu_0 = 4 pi K / c^2,

the Biot-Savart field of a slowly moving charge, with the compendium's `mu_0`. For many bodies the fields add.

**Symbolic check** (`team/notes/E2.2_check.py`, SymPy, away from the body): `div B_cal = 0`; `curl B_cal = (1/c^2) dE_cal/dt`; `div E_cal = 0`; `curl E_cal + dB_cal/dt` is of order `(v/c)^2`; `B = -s^2 b` is Biot-Savart with `mu_0 = 4 pi K / c^2`. All hold.

## What it means, and its limits (for the physics lead)

- **The Composer part is ordinary magnetism:** two moving charges feel `q v x B` with Biot-Savart's `B`. Nothing new; it is what L0 must give, and it can be checked against the textbook.
- **The Real part is a gravitomagnetic field, `B_g = (1/c^2) v x g`.** This is the field behind the paper's stated failure: frame dragging around the Earth (LAGEOS) comes out at a quarter of the measured value (compendium, "Where the law fails"). The engine will compute it faithfully, and every result that depends on `B_g` from moving masses must say so.
- **First order in `v/c`:** no retardation, no radiation, and the force between two moving bodies does not obey Newton's third law at this order (as in ordinary electromagnetism, where the missing momentum is in the field). Scenarios that test the third law (T1.6) must not use moving-body fields, or must account for this.
- **Units:** in scaled units the result depends on `c`, which is still question 6 (the engine assumes Stoney units, `c = e = 1`).

## Implementation plan (after approval)

- `fields/direct.py`: the field at body `i` from the other bodies gains `B_g_i = (1/c^2) sum_j v_j x g_ij` and `b_i = (1/c^2) sum_j v_j x e_ij`, with the same softening and blocks as `g` and `e`. Fixed bodies count as at rest. In 2D the second field is the component out of the plane, `v_x g_y - v_y g_x`. Uniform backgrounds are still added unchanged.
- An option, so that every existing result stays reproducible: the moving-body field is on only when a scenario asks for it (`numerics` or `universe`; the name is to be agreed), and runs say which they used.
- **Done when** (`docs/WORK-PLAN.md`, E2.2): one moving proton gives the analytic slow-body field within 1e-6, and `B = -s^2 b`; plus two parallel moving charges feel the textbook magnetic force, and the reduce-to-L0 and T0 gates still pass.
