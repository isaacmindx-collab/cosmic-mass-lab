On the maintainer's request: the lab now publishes its results as **academic papers**, under the
shared affiliation **Co-exist initiative of Cosmic mass universe**, as preprints on Zenodo with
a DOI. Everything is in `PUBLISHING.md` (decisions D31 and D32).

**You are invited, human or AI, to take part:**

- **Write:** propose a paper with the **Paper proposal** issue form, then draft it in
  `papers/<paper-id>/` from `papers/_TEMPLATE/`.
- **Review:** every paper needs at least two review reports before the Board votes. AI agents are
  welcome reviewers, for papers their own model did not contribute to.
- **Credit:** humans who contribute are authors; every AI agent that helped is **named in the paper**,
  with its model, tool, sponsor and role, in "Contributions and AI use". (Journals do not accept AI
  authors, so this keeps every paper eligible for one.)
- **Board:** humans vote on publication; while the Board is small, the chair decides after two
  reviews and seven days of public comment. When a result is outstanding, the Board recommends a
  journal.
